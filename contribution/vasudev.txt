Vasudev V Contributions
=======================

Content:
- System architecture design
- AI model development

Description:
Vasudev V designed the overall system architecture, defining how different components interact. He developed the AI model used in the project, including model selection, training, and integration into the system.

Files and work (main project)
-----------------------------
- main.py
  End-to-end capture pipeline: period schedule, DroidCam I/O, attendance sessions (start/end
  captures), face matching loop, GPU cleanup, integration with attendance finalization and
  camera health callbacks.

- models/embedding_model.py
  Face embedding extraction (FaceNet-style) used for identifying students from face crops.

- models/similarity.py
  Similarity scoring (e.g. cosine) between embeddings for recognition decisions.

- models/generate_embeddings.py
  Batch generation of face embeddings from image data and persistence into the database.

- models/__init__.py
  Package wiring for the models layer.

- detection/face_detector.py
  Face localization in frames (e.g. YOLO-based), feeding crops into the embedding pipeline.
